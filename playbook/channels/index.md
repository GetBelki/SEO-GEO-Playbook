# Channels

Channels beyond your own pages: product feeds and Merchant Center, third-party presence, local.

* [Feeds and Merchant Center](feeds-and-merchant-center.md) - Feed audit checklist for Shopping and AI product cards — plain-word attributes, specific categories, data-source hygiene, feed-only title tests, feed links as landing URLs, promotions, reviews — plus constraint copy that answers "will it fit?", consistent product-line naming, Merchant Center limits, GTIN rules, price matching and the 2026 attribute and API changes.
* [Google Discover](google-discover.md) - Discover as a bonus channel for a shop's editorial and guide content, never a baseline — what it rewards (large images, strong headlines), its volatility and losses during core updates, the February 2026 Discover core update, why hreflang doesn't apply, and tracking it separately in Search Console.
* [Keyword research](keyword-research.md) - How far to trust search volume, valuing topics by the traffic of the page that ranks, long-tail and zero-volume keywords, multi-facet intent, clustering and keyword-to-URL mapping, secondary keywords, customer vocabulary and complementary sub-needs, seeding AI prompts from Search Console, how reliable AI classification is, and support-type volume as a bug report.
* [Links and digital PR](links-and-digital-pr.md) - What links still do and what Domain Rating doesn't, ecommerce link tactics without paid links, original statistics pages, data-led and regional PR, pitching the publishers and URLs AI assistants cite, unlinked mentions as implied links, why not to disavow tool-flagged "toxic" links, monthly link reclamation and a quarterly mention audit.
* [Local search and Maps](local.md) - Local visibility in Maps and AI answers — the business profile's primary category and identity data, category diversity in local results, the business website as the most-cited local source, directories (Foursquare, Yelp, Bing Places) as verification and ChatGPT's Foursquare dependence, reviews and which ones get shown, photos, local-inventory results, Gemini in Maps, and profiles and location pages for multi-location retail.
* [Third-party presence](third-party-presence.md) - Why most AI citations come from third parties — known sources, authorities and testing organisations, affiliate and review sites, marketplaces, Reddit (which differs by engine), forums and YouTube — what drives brand mentions, Knowledge Graph corroboration, and what to do: audit cited domains, keep facts identical across domains, publish verifiable proof points, earn need-to-brand statements and mentions with proprietary data, keep an active review-platform profile.
