# Third-party presence

Why AI answers mostly cite sites other than yours, which third parties carry weight, and what you can do about it. Confidence labels follow the [scale](../reference/sources-and-confidence.md).

## Rules
- **Audit which third-party domains are cited for your category prompts**, check that you are listed there with correct facts, and prioritise the work by evidence role and durability, not by which platform is cited this month ([section 15](#15-prioritise-by-evidence-role-not-by-what-gets-cited-today)). *Measured* — [evidence](#2-most-citations-are-third-party), [what to do](#6-what-to-do)
- **Keep facts identical across marketplaces, review sites, listings, feed and site**, because stale or conflicting listings undercut your own claims, and the Knowledge Graph accepts a fact only when other domains corroborate it. *Inference; Patent* — [evidence](#2-most-citations-are-third-party), [corroboration](#11-knowledge-graph-facts-need-corroboration-from-other-domains)
- **Before cutting affiliate or roundup listings, check whether they are cited** for your category prompts. *Measured; the effect of cutting is inference* — [evidence](#2-most-citations-are-third-party)
- **Aim to be named on the few trusted lists, guides and comparison pages per category** rather than tuning your own pages further — including the pages that rank for "<category> + <authority>", the reviewers and testing bodies models name. Re-baseline at every model change. *Inference; Measured, small samples* — [evidence](#1-assistants-prefer-sources-they-already-know), [authorities](#10-authorities-testing-bodies-and-model-changes)
- **Publish verifiable proof points as text on the homepage** (review score with count, years trading, assortment size, awards). *Measured, mid-authority brands* — [evidence](#3-proof-points-and-topical-focus)
- **Don't add schema markup expecting more citations**; keep it for rich results. *Measured, vendor data* — [evidence](#2-most-citations-are-third-party)
- **Treat each market's leading marketplace as a citation and corroboration channel.** *Measured* — [evidence](#4-marketplaces-per-market)
- **Earn mentions and links with proprietary data, tools and guides**, and be present on Reddit and YouTube where answers cite them. *Measured correlation; the tactic is inference* — [evidence](#5-link-assets), [what to do](#6-what-to-do)
- **Monitor and openly take part in Reddit and Facebook Groups threads for your category — but invest in forums only where they already rank in Google for your queries**; ChatGPT cut Reddit citations by about 95 % in August 2026. *Measured (prevalence), vendor data; the tactic is inference* — [evidence](#7-forums-and-discussions), [by engine](#12-reddit-differs-by-engine)
- **Build brand demand, not only rankings**: ChatGPT adds known brand names to its searches, and branded mentions correlate with AI visibility more than links do. Publish and earn "need/category → brand" statements, the direction models recall worst. *Measured correlation, vendor data; Relayed; one weak case* — [evidence](#8-what-gets-a-brand-mentioned), [case](#9-ranking-well-is-not-ai-visibility), [recall](#13-need-to-brand-statements)
- **Keep an active profile on the review platforms your market's answers cite**, with a steady flow of reviews and regular replies; brands without a profile almost never appear in answers that cite the platform. *Measured, sponsored vendor study, relayed* — [evidence](#14-review-platform-profiles)

## 1. Assistants prefer sources they already know
- **Known sources win.** The model prefers sources it knows from training.[^chatgpt-53] Being named on the few trusted lists, guides and comparison pages per category likely matters more than on-page tweaks. *Inference.*
- **Few categories have an owner yet.** In ChatGPT, 15.2 % of 1,094 US categories had a clear owning brand and 53.7 % were open; owners kept first place in 90.4 % of month-over-month comparisons. *Measured, correlational, ChatGPT only.*[^gm-owners]

## 2. Most citations are third-party
- **Most citations are third-party.** For ecommerce, owned sites made up 30.4 % of AI citations and external sources 69.6 % (earned 42.0 %).[^aleyda-citations] For about 1,000 bottom-funnel prompts (pricing, "vs", "best X for Y"), third parties held "80-95% of AI citations"; comparison pages were the most-cited URL type, then listicles, while product pages and homepages were cited at single-digit rates. Reddit appeared in ~62 % of responses, YouTube in ~25 %. *Measured, vendor data, ecommerce not broken out.*[^siege-bofu] (Jan–Feb 2026 data; ChatGPT has since cut Reddit citations sharply — [section 12](#12-reddit-differs-by-engine).) Stale or conflicting third-party listings undercut your own claims.
- **Affiliate and review sites carry weight.** 52.5 % of the 40 most-cited domains for bottom-funnel prompts were affiliate or sponsorship sites. *Measured; the claim that cutting affiliate spend erodes visibility is inference.*[^siege-leader] Before dropping roundup or affiliate listings, check whether they are cited for your category prompts.
- **Brand and third-party signals correlate with AI visibility.** Across 75,000 brands with DR > 40, AI visibility correlated most with YouTube mentions (~0.737) and branded web mentions (AI Overviews 0.656, AI Mode 0.709, ChatGPT 0.664), then branded search volume (0.352–0.466); Domain Rating correlated at 0.266–0.326 and backlinks only "very weak". *Measured, vendor data, Spearman; skews to big brands, and the factors aren't independent.*[^ah-brand-corr] A branded-anchor correlation of 0.6 is relayed by Growth Memo; it isn't among the figures read from the original study. *Relayed.*[^gm-2027]
- **Adding schema did not raise AI citations.** 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD (Aug 2025 – Mar 2026) were compared with ~4,000 matched controls, 30 days before and after (difference-in-differences): AI Overview citations −4.6 % (significant, but the pre-trends were already declining), AI Mode +2.4 % and ChatGPT +2.2 % (indistinguishable from zero). Cited pages were ~3× more likely to carry JSON-LD — a correlation only. *Measured, vendor data.*[^ah-schema] Keep schema for rich results. Until 2026-10-28 this page cited a Thrive-relayed schema correlation of "−0.04 to +0.04 (Ahrefs)"; Ahrefs never published it — its 0.04 Spearman is word count vs AI Overview citation.
- **Relevance over authority.** Thrive relays semantic relevance as the strongest citation driver and domain authority correlating at −0.10 to −0.21 (SearchAtlas, 104,855 citations). *Relayed.*[^thrive-sources]

## 3. Proof points and topical focus
- **Verifiable proof points help mid-sized brands.** Mid-authority brands with 5+ verifiable proof points on the homepage (review score with count, years trading, assortment size, awards — each naming a source or figure) had "62% more AI visibility" than peers with none; most of the gain came from the first two, none for high-authority brands. *Measured, observational, 585 brands.*[^siege-credibility] Put them in text, not only as badge images.
- **Cited ≠ recommended.** The most-cited domain was the most-mentioned brand only 20.8 % of the time. Brands are cited across many topics but recommended mainly in their core ones (in close categories 74 % of appearances were citations, 44 % mentions; in distant ones 50 % and 25 %).[^gm-owners][^gm-focus] Topical depth beats breadth — the same idea as Google's site focus ([how Google ranks](../how-it-works/google-ranking.md)).

## 4. Marketplaces per market
- **Marketplaces are the AI shopping destinations.** In each market, a few marketplaces take a large share of AI ecommerce clicks — a global one in some markets, a local champion in others. *Measured, Similarweb, one month; search extracts.*[^aleyda-global] Figures per market are in [shopping surfaces](../how-it-works/shopping-surfaces.md). Treat each market's leading marketplace as a citation and corroboration channel, and keep your listing there consistent with your feed ([feeds](feeds-and-merchant-center.md)).

## 5. Link assets
- **These are also the link assets.** Proprietary data, tools and calculators, buyer's guides and niche checklists earn links naturally; ecommerce sites earned about 8.7 new referring domains per 10,000 organic visits. *Measured, 100 sites, tool undisclosed.*[^siege-links] The same pages are the ones assistants cite most ([comparison and guides](../page-types/comparison-and-guides.md)).

## 6. What to do
- **Audit the cited third parties.** List every domain cited for your category prompts — marketplaces, review sites, affiliates, roundups, forums, video — and check whether you're listed and whether the facts match ([third-party citation audit](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md#14-third-party-citation-audit), using [prompt tracking](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md#9-prompt-tracking-per-engine)). Supported by [section 2](#2-most-citations-are-third-party).
- **Keep listings consistent.** Price range, assortment, returns, delivery and location should read the same on every listing you control, in the feed and on your site. *Inference from [section 2](#2-most-citations-are-third-party).*
- **Reddit and YouTube.** Both appear in a large share of bottom-funnel answers, and YouTube mentions correlate with AI Overview visibility ([section 2](#2-most-citations-are-third-party)). Answer real questions in relevant communities and publish product and use videos. *Inference; no study here tests the tactic.*
- **Digital PR via proprietary data.** Publish data, tools and guides others will cite and link to ([section 5](#5-link-assets)); aim for the category lists and comparisons that assistants already trust ([section 1](#1-assistants-prefer-sources-they-already-know)). Pitch lists, data-led PR and ecommerce link tactics: [links and digital PR](links-and-digital-pr.md). *Inference.*
- **Mention audit.** Record a baseline of brand mentions and track lost or weak ones quarterly; look for pages that mention several competitors but not you; fix outdated directory listings that assistants misread. Method in [links and digital PR](links-and-digital-pr.md). *Practitioner, vendor promotion.*[^ah-mention-audit]
- **Local businesses:** directories and business profiles act as verification for local answers ([local](local.md)).

## 7. Forums and discussions
- **Forum threads sit on commercial results pages.** Of 145M US results pages, 11.7 % showed the Discussions and Forums feature, and 40.9 % of those had commercial intent. Reddit appeared in 87.8 % of the results pages with the feature (83.9 % worldwide); Reddit, Facebook and Quora held 76.75 % of its links. Backlinks to the threads showed no correlation with their ranking. *Measured, vendor data.*[^ah-forums]
- **AI Mode cites the same platforms.** In US AI Mode, reddit.com took 17.9 % and youtube.com 17.8 % of citations; amazon.com was the top retailer at 3.0 %. *Vendor data; sample not disclosed, report generated by an AI agent.*[^ah-ai-mode-domains]
- **Do:** monitor Reddit and Facebook Groups threads for your category and take part openly, as the brand, with useful answers. *Inference; no study here tests the tactic.* Prioritise threads that already rank in Google for your queries; Reddit's weight differs sharply by engine ([section 12](#12-reddit-differs-by-engine)).

## 8. What gets a brand mentioned
A synthesis of third-party studies, *relayed* — each number comes from the named study, not from the synthesiser:[^bp-mentions]
- **Perplexity cites more sources than ChatGPT.** 21.87 vs 7.92 citations per question (Qwairy); 16.35 vs 6.88 per prompt (arXiv 2604.25707).
- **ChatGPT leans on institutions.** 79 % of ChatGPT retrievals went to corporate or institutional domains (Peec, 1,500 chats); editorial and reference sites made up 29 % of Perplexity's sources vs 9 % of ChatGPT's.
- **Relevance and retrieval position decide who is cited first.** In a 252,000-trial study, topical relevance and position in the retrieved results had the strongest effect; explicit pricing and recent timestamps helped.
- **Known brands get searched for.** ChatGPT adds known brand names to its fan-out queries, so brands the model doesn't know are left out before ranking matters. Same direction as [section 1](#1-assistants-prefer-sources-they-already-know).

## 9. Ranking well is not AI visibility
- **One weak case.** Two beauty direct-to-consumer brands with the same domain authority (57): one had more keywords (108,700 vs 71,200), indexed pages (30,000 vs 2,732) and backlinks (54,200 vs 20,300); the other had more branded visits (767,000 vs 336,000) and the higher Semrush AI visibility score (77 vs 52). *Practitioner, two brands, method undisclosed, approximate figures.*[^bp-seo-vs-ai] It fits the brand-mention correlations in [section 2](#2-most-citations-are-third-party) but proves nothing on its own.

## 10. Authorities, testing bodies and model changes
- **A model change can flip whether a brand is mentioned.** For one prompt, the same brand was mentioned in 1 of 7 runs on Gemini 3.5 Flash and 7 of 7 on a newer Gemini model, on the same day with the same pages (p = 0.005). The newer model added a consumer-testing organisation's name to its fan-out query, which surfaced a retailer's "ranking per <organisation>" page naming the brand. Across 4 more categories, 9 brands went from ≤ 10 % of answers without search to ≥ 40 % with search, always through a third-party page. AI Mode showed the brand in 0 of 20 runs. *Measured, small samples, Gemini only.*[^nz-model]
- **Authorities are often misquoted.** Only 37 % (58 of 158) of answers whose searches named an authority cited that authority's own site; retailer and affiliate "<authority> best buy" pages often misquote it.[^nz-model]
- **Do:** per category, ask current models without search which reviewers and testing bodies they trust; get covered on the pages that rank for "<category> + <authority>"; re-baseline your tracking at every model change ([AI visibility tracking](../measure/ai-visibility-tracking.md)). Don't publish "according to X" rankings featuring your own products; if no authority covers you, publish a dated, self-labelled comparison that discloses its method ([comparison and guides](../page-types/comparison-and-guides.md)). *Practitioner, following the measurements above.*[^nz-model]

## 11. Knowledge Graph facts need corroboration from other domains
- A Google patent describes Knowledge Graph reconciliation: extracted facts are merged only when corroborated; for single-answer facts (founding date and the like) conflicting values are discarded; several sources from one domain may not be enough. *Patent; current use unknown.*[^sbs-kgrecon]
- **Do:** keep brand and product facts identical on your own site, Wikidata, marketplaces and in press coverage, so they corroborate each other across domains. How Knowledge Graph facts reach Google's AI answers: [Google AI features](../how-it-works/google-ai-features.md); the Google-Extended question: [AI crawlers and controls](../technical/ai-crawlers-and-controls.md).

## 12. Reddit differs by engine
- **ChatGPT dropped Reddit.** Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations fell from ~4.6 % to 0.23 % between early August and 21 August 2026 (−95 %), in steps on 8 and 14 August; Google's AI surfaces were not affected the same way. *Measured, large vendor log sample (Peec AI, the author's employer), not reproducible outside it.*[^mt-sitequeries]
- **Reddit follows search, not AI preference.** OpenAI selected 0.61 % of 491,024 Reddit retrievals vs 12.85 % for all sources; Reddit made up 2.0 % of 697,768 Google AI citations and appeared in 26.6 % of a 3,450-search sample; it was 0 of 139,601 Anthropic grounding sources. *Measured, biased to the author's own prompts.*[^dj-reddit]
- **Translated Reddit in non-English markets.** Machine-translated Reddit pages (`?tl=` URLs) took 5.71 % of Reddit's AI citations across 20 countries and 4 engines (2026-03-01 – 06-10): in Germany 52 % of Google AI Overviews' Reddit citations, in Spain 72 % of AI Mode's. ChatGPT dropped them from 6.14 % (April) to 0.30 % (1–10 June; Germany 18.06 % → 1.54 %); Perplexity cited none. In Germany they clustered in commercial categories (bags/fashion 13.0 %, tech hardware 12.7 %, audio/smart home 11.6 %). *Measured, vendor data (Peec AI).*[^mt-reddit-tl]
- **Do:** invest in forum work only where forum threads already rank in Google for your queries; weight ChatGPT-oriented third-party work toward review platforms and official sources; in non-English markets, treat translated English Reddit threads as competitors for product queries in Google's AI surfaces. Recheck monthly — these shares moved within weeks. *Inference from the measurements above.*
- The studies disagree on how much engines cite Reddit at all (from ~62 % of bottom-funnel responses to 2.0 % of Google AI citations); the figures and why they differ: [conflicts](../reference/conflicts.md#how-much-do-ai-engines-cite-reddit).

## 13. Need-to-brand statements
- A Google Research study (13 LLMs, 2,150 facts), summarised by DEJAN: models encode 95–98 % of facts but fail to recall 26–34 %; long-tail recall is 20 to 35 points lower; reverse queries (feature → brand) are weaker than brand → feature; thinking modes recover 40–65 % of missed facts. *Research study, relayed.*[^dj-parametric]
- **Do:** publish and earn statements in the "need/category → brand" direction ("for <use>, <brand> …"), on your own pages and in third-party coverage, because that is the direction an assistant has to recall when a shopper asks by need. *Inference.*

## 14. Review-platform profiles
- In a study Seer ran for Trustpilot (~800,000 AI responses, May 2026), brands without a Trustpilot profile appeared in a median 1 % of the answers that cited Trustpilot; with a minimal profile 53.5 %; with 80+ reviews and regular replies to them ~75 % or more. *Measured, vendor data, sponsored by the platform it measures; relayed by a practitioner, primary not read.*[^ls-context]
- **Do:** find out which review platforms the answers in your category and market cite (the [third-party citation audit](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md#14-third-party-citation-audit)); claim the profile there, keep collecting reviews and reply to them regularly, and keep its facts identical to your site ([section 6](#6-what-to-do)). *Inference from the study above; the platform funded it, so treat the size of the effect with caution.*

## 15. Prioritise by evidence role, not by what gets cited today
- **The external share is large in every vertical, but the sources differ.** In one consultant's analysis of the most-cited sources for SaaS, ecommerce and finance, external domains made up 69.6–82.3 % of the top cited sources. For ecommerce the most influential external sources were marketplaces and competitor domains; social and community sources mattered most for SaaS, and specialist publishers, reviews and comparison sites for finance. *Measured; sample sizes not stated on this page.*[^aleyda-3p]
- **Score each kind of third-party presence on six criteria:** how strongly it shapes relevant answers (seen repeatedly across your prompt groups, not just because the domain is big), whether it ties the brand to a specific need, whether a marketing team already owns it, its business value along the customer journey, whether it keeps paying off if the engines change what they cite, and the effort it takes. Treat the scores as starting points and check them against your own prompt library and citations. *Practitioner framework.*[^aleyda-3p]
- **Worth investing in, for a shop:** complete and consistent marketplace, retailer and reseller listings; independent hands-on reviews and tests; evidence-led comparisons and shortlists with disclosed criteria; detailed customer reviews on maintained profiles; how-to demonstrations (often video); coverage of your own data in specialist media; authentic answers in community threads. Each needs an owner: marketplace or ecommerce team, digital PR, creator marketing, reviews and reputation, community. *Practitioner framework.*[^aleyda-3p]
- **Minimise or avoid:** passing mentions and logo walls, reach-first coverage on irrelevant big sites, syndicated press releases (more domains, no new corroboration), thin or paid listicles without method, sponsored posts repeating brand claims, mass directory submissions, stale or contradictory listings (fix the influential ones instead), content made only because one platform is cited right now, and bulk AI-generated articles or reviews. Fake reviews, astroturfing and undisclosed endorsements are out entirely. *Practitioner framework.*[^aleyda-3p]
- **Do:** list the third-party presences you have or could build, score them with your marketing teams, and fund the ones that would still help customers if the engines stopped citing them. See [links and digital PR](links-and-digital-pr.md) for earning coverage.
