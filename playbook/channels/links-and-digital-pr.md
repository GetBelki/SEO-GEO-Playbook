# Links and digital PR

What links still do for an ecommerce site, which link tactics work without buying links, and how PR and mention work feed AI visibility. Mentions, forums and the third-party sites AI answers cite are on [third-party presence](third-party-presence.md). Confidence labels follow the [scale](../reference/sources-and-confidence.md). Most sources here are Ahrefs, a link-data vendor; sections 2, 4 and 8 add a practitioner case, a practitioner method and a Google patent.

## Rules
- **Benchmark referring domains and authority against the sites ranking for your target SERPs**, not against an absolute Domain Rating; DR isn't a Google factor. *Vendor data* — [evidence](#1-what-links-do-and-what-dr-doesnt)
- **Vary anchor text; don't build exact-match anchor patterns.** *Practitioner* — [evidence](#1-what-links-do-and-what-dr-doesnt)
- **Earn ecommerce links from roundups, the brands you stock and statistics pages** built on original, sourced data, never by paying for followed links. *Practitioner, dated 2022; one 2026 case* — [evidence](#2-ecommerce-link-tactics)
- **Run data-led PR: public data turned into a regional comparison, pitched to local journalists.** *Practitioner, 3 campaigns* — [evidence](#3-data-led-and-regional-pr)
- **Build pitch lists from the publishers and URLs AI assistants cite in your vertical, and pitch at publisher-group level.** *Measured, vendor data; Practitioner* — [evidence](#4-pitch-the-publishers-ai-assistants-cite)
- **Don't disavow links because a tool calls them "toxic"**; disavow only against a manual action risk from many artificial links. *Official (Google statements), relayed* — [evidence](#5-dont-disavow-tool-flagged-toxic-links)
- **Monthly, 301 the most-linked 404 URLs to their closest live equivalent.** *Practitioner, one domain* — [evidence](#6-link-reclamation)
- **Audit brand mentions quarterly**: lost or weak mentions, pages naming competitors but not you, outdated listings. *Practitioner, vendor promo* — [evidence](#7-mention-audit)
- **Count unlinked brand mentions and brand-query growth alongside links**, especially in a quality recovery. *Patent* — [evidence](#8-unlinked-mentions-as-implied-links)

## 1. What links do and what DR doesn't
- Domain Rating correlated with rankings at 0.131 in a 2025 Ahrefs study whose method isn't described. DR is Ahrefs' metric, not a Google factor. Its published benchmarks cover SaaS only (600 sites, median 62, top quartile 77). *Vendor data.*[^ah-dr]
- **Do:** compare yourself with the DR and referring domains of the sites that rank for your target SERPs, and grow referring domains rather than chasing a DR number.[^ah-dr]
- **Links vs mentions in AI answers.** For one brand's 31,000+ AI mentions, the mention carried a link in 10.7 % of AI Overview answers, 16.8 % Gemini, 26.9 % ChatGPT, 36.8 % AI Mode and 51.6 % Perplexity (~28 % overall). *Measured, single brand.*[^ah-mentions-links] Most AI visibility arrives without a link, and brand-mention correlations with AI visibility are stronger than link correlations ([third-party presence](third-party-presence.md#2-most-citations-are-third-party)).
- **Anchor text.** Exact-match anchor patterns and keyword stuffing still risk manual actions; Ahrefs ranks #1 for "canonical tagging" without the phrase on the page. *Practitioner.*[^ah-overopt] Google's link spam policy: [technical foundations](../technical/technical-foundations.md#11-links-and-spam-policies).

## 2. Ecommerce link tactics
*Practitioner, dated 2022 with a 2024 roundup.*[^ah-ecom-links]
- **"Best X" listicles.** Offer a product sample to the author for feedback, not in exchange for a link.
- **Stockist pages.** Ask the brands you stock to list you on their "where to buy" or stockist pages.
- **Unlinked brand mentions.** Ask sites that already name you to add the link (find them in the [mention audit](#7-mention-audit)).
- **Statistics pages.** One agency reports an average of 102 referring domains per statistics page (its own research); Ahrefs' own statistics page had 37 backlinks.
- **Statistics pages also get cited by AI.** LLMs add "statistics" and the current year to research fan-out queries. In one case, a statistics site with no verified data got ~72,000 ChatGPT-User requests out of 600k+ bot hits in 30 days, 10k+ ChatGPT sessions and 330+ referring domains, despite a Google demotion. *Practitioner, one site; the author works at an AI-visibility vendor (Peec AI).*[^mt-stats] The case shows the demand, not a method to copy: **do** publish genuinely original, sourced data (for example a yearly "[category] statistics" page) with the source and year next to each number.
- Paid links must carry `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"` ([technical foundations](../technical/technical-foundations.md#11-links-and-spam-policies)).

## 3. Data-led and regional PR
- The "tabloid technique": take public or freedom-of-information data, turn it into a regional comparison (city A vs city B), and pitch each region's result to local journalists.[^ah-tabloid]
- Three cases: 58 or 59 referring domains (the article gives both), 188 sites, and 72 referring domains. No success rate is given, and the author says "No technique works every time." *Practitioner, 3 campaigns.*[^ah-tabloid]
- For ecommerce, the data can come from your own sales or search trends, anonymised and aggregated — the same proprietary data that earns links and citations ([third-party presence](third-party-presence.md#5-link-assets)). *Inference.*

## 4. Pitch the publishers AI assistants cite
- Of the 100 domains ChatGPT cited most (9.6M queries), 54 % accept pitches. Among the top 50 pitchable ones, 16 belong to one publisher group; parent-group shares of the top 50: People Inc 43 %, Condé Nast 16 %, Future plc 11 %, News Corp 11 %, Hearst 8 %. *Measured, vendor data, US-weighted.*[^ah-pr-chatgpt]
- **Do:** build the pitch list from the publishers AI assistants cite for your category prompts ([AI visibility tracking](../measure/ai-visibility-tracking.md)), and pitch at publisher-group level, because groups syndicate across titles.[^ah-pr-chatgpt]
- **Pitch the URLs, not only the publishers.** Mine the specific URLs AI engines cite repeatedly for your prompts, and pitch or correct those pages; aim for natural links with plain anchors. Planted mentions are probably devalued — a forecast, not a measurement. *Practitioner.*[^dj-outreach]

## 5. Don't disavow tool-flagged toxic links
- Google has "no notion" of toxic links (Mueller). Disavow only when you have "a considerable number of spammy, artificial, or low-quality links" that caused or will likely cause a manual action. Illyes found no case where negative SEO really caused a drop. *Official statements, relayed by a vendor.*[^ah-toxic]
- In a poll, 38 % of SEOs still disavow. Anecdotes: one site lost 60 % after a tool-driven disavow; Ahrefs' own test disavow caused a dip that reversed. *Anecdotes.*[^ah-toxic]

## 6. Link reclamation
- Ahrefs' domain lost 6,285 links in 30 days; 594 passed a "best links" filter. A third-party outreach case reclaimed 31 of 166 emailed links (18.67 %). Lost links from linking pages that now redirect are "rarely worth pursuing". *Practitioner, one domain.*[^ah-reclamation]
- **Do:** monthly, list your 404 URLs with the most referring domains and 301 each to its closest live equivalent; ask for corrections only on the best lost links.[^ah-reclamation] Retired products: see [product pages](../page-types/product-pages.md).

## 7. Mention audit
- Set a baseline of brand mentions and track lost or weakened ones quarterly; find pages that mention several competitors but not you; fix outdated directory listings that AI answers misread. *Practitioner, vendor promotion.*[^ah-mention-audit]
- Pages that mention you without linking feed [section 2](#2-ecommerce-link-tactics); listings and cited third parties are covered in [third-party presence](third-party-presence.md#6-what-to-do).

## 8. Unlinked mentions as implied links
- A Google patent (the "Panda" patent) computes, per group of pages (a site, subdomain or directory), the ratio of independent links — express links plus *implied* links, i.e. references to the site without a hyperlink — to reference queries (searches that refer to the site). The ratio becomes a modification factor applied to every page in the group. *Patent; how much of this runs today is unknown.*[^sbs-panda]
- **Do:** in a quality recovery, compare growth in unlinked brand mentions and in brand queries, not only in links ([core-update recovery](../situations/core-update-recovery.md)). The [mention audit](#7-mention-audit) finds the mentions; asking for the link ([section 2](#2-ecommerce-link-tactics)) is optional on top. *Inference.*
