# SEO / GEO Playbook

A brand-neutral, ecommerce-first reference for search (SEO), AI-search visibility (GEO) and the measurement behind both. It distils vendor documentation (Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Apple, Bing), reverse-engineering research (Resoneo), measured studies (Growth Memo, Siege Media, Aleyda Solis), trial exhibits and Google statements (Marie Haynes), retrieval mechanics and measurement method (iPullRank), large vendor studies (Ahrefs), technical case work (Botpresso), performance (web.dev, DebugBear), behavioural analytics (FullStory), clickstream and AI-consistency studies (SparkToro), ML methods (ML for SEO), a vendor ranking model (SERPrecon), recorded AI search sessions (Steve Toth), leak, Chromium and grounding experiments (Natzir Turrado, DEJAN, Metehan Yeşilyurt), patents (SEO by the Sea), practitioner methods (Bruce Clay), agency and consultant studies and cases (Seer Interactive, Glenn Gabe, Lazarina Stoy, Jono Alderson, Barry Adams, Lily Ray, Jes Scholz, Crystal Carter), GA4 and tag-manager troubleshooting (Analytics Mania), dated SERP sightings (Brodie Clark), Google's update history and practitioner write-ups (Searchind; studies relayed by Thrive and Ignite Visibility), checked against two years of one shop's data. It holds no site, brand or product specifics, so any project can copy it or link to it.

**Start here:** [the playbook on one page](start-here.md) — 51 rules, each linked to its evidence.
