## Google's guidance vs assistant findings
- **Google:** you "don't need to write in a specific way just for generative AI search"; "no requirement to break your content into tiny pieces"; no need for "new machine readable files, AI text files, markup, or Markdown" — it "is ... still SEO". *Official.*[^google-ai-guide]
- **Assistants, measured:** ChatGPT's model sees the title, the URL and a ~202-character snippet from the H1, and ignores the meta description. *Measured.*[^chatgpt-retrieval] Microsoft asks for liftable Q&A blocks and no answers hidden in tabs for Copilot. *Official, for Microsoft.*[^msft-ai-answers]
- **Reading:** each vendor is describing its own system. Follow Google's guidance for Google's surfaces and the measured behaviour for each assistant.
- **Do:** fact-first, self-contained writing in visible HTML satisfies all of them ([AI assistants](../how-it-works/ai-assistants.md), [Google AI features](../how-it-works/google-ai-features.md)).

## Writing for embeddings and chunking
- **Against:** Google's John Mueller — optimising for embeddings "is literally keywords stuffing"; pages restructured into micro-intent sections gained first and then lost much of it. *Statement plus anecdotes.*[^mh-vector] Google's 2026 guide: no chunking requirement.[^google-ai-guide]
- **For:** iPullRank — retrieval scores passages by vector similarity; splitting a mixed paragraph raised cosine similarity from 0.541 to 0.645 in its own test, and adding a heading by 17.54 %; Bing's documentation says chunking "must preserve meaning and claims". *One test, its own tool.*[^ipr-chunking] Its wider "relevance engineering" claims — Google builds site and author vectors, 50–150-word paragraphs, score pages by cosine similarity before publishing — are uncited.[^ipr-relevance]
- **In between:** Aleyda Solis — make sections self-contained with the direct answer first, without embedding-tuning claims. *Practitioner, search extracts.*[^aleyda-ai-vs-trad]
- **Do:** one idea per passage under a descriptive heading, entity named early. Don't tune text to similarity scores; judge any rewrite by whether rankings hold over weeks ([Google ranking](../how-it-works/google-ranking.md)).

## Does AI traffic convert better?
- **Much better:** ChatGPT sessions converted at 13.8 % vs 3.02 % for Google organic on 55 sites. *Measured, one author, method not published.*[^mh-geo]
- **About the same:** median AI-to-organic conversion ratio 1.26× across 78 GA4 properties; ecommerce 1.01×, AI ahead on 56 % of sites. *Measured, method published.*[^siege-conversion]
- **Worse:** an SSRN paper (5585812) on 50,000+ ChatGPT-referred transactions across nearly 1,000 ecommerce sites found ChatGPT referrals converted worse and earned less per visit than every major channel except paid social, at "under 0.2% of total traffic". *Measured, relayed by Bruce Clay; read the paper before quoting.*[^bc-llm-vs-search]
- **More data, still split:** Adobe reports AI referrals converting ~42 % better and Loamly 10.2 % vs 2.5 %, while SEOWorks found AI converting worse than organic on 23 of 63 sites. *Measured, relayed; vendor data.*[^sp-ai-boondoggle][^mlseo-dark-ai]
- **Do:** assume parity at best for ecommerce until your own data says otherwise; report AI conversion against organic per vertical ([AI visibility tracking](../measure/ai-visibility-tracking.md)).

## Is GEO worth the effort?
- **No (for now):** ChatGPT brought 0.62 % as many sessions as Google organic, all assistants 0.73 %; citation tracking "is a distraction". *Measured plus opinion.*[^mh-geo]
- **Yes:** 88 % of AI Mode shoppers took the AI shortlist as given and 74 % picked the top product. *Measured, 185 tasks.*[^gm-highstakes] Judging AI search by referral clicks is "a category error": ChatGPT mentions brands 3.2× more often than it cites them. *Relayed.*[^ipr-brand]
- **Reading:** both can hold — the traffic is small, but the decision may be made before the click.
- **Do:** weigh effort by your own assistant share and conversion; read branded search alongside it ([diagnostic tests](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md#11-monthly-read-exposure-behaviour-outcomes)).

## Is AI traffic hiding in Direct?
- **Mechanism exists:** about 95 % of links ChatGPT displays carry `utm_source=chatgpt.com`; pages the model opened itself never do. *Measured.*[^chatgpt-retrieval]
- **Large share claimed:** iPullRank cites an estimate that about 70 % of AI traffic lands as Direct, and correlations of AI share of voice with Direct of 0.72 and 0.47. *Relayed, older.*[^ipr-brand]
- **Small share measured:** in 12 GA4 properties, AI-traced sessions were 0.003 % of Direct; the correlations failed placebo and split-period tests; Direct spikes were mostly bots. *Measured, later.*[^gm-direct]
- **A higher estimate:** Loamly reports 70.6 % of identified AI visits arriving without a referrer. *Vendor data.*[^mlseo-dark-ai]
- **Do:** use the tested result; treat the analytics AI line as a floor; rule out bots before explaining a Direct rise ([diagnostic tests](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md#10-is-a-direct-spike-real)).

## Short or long pages?
- **Short:** shorter, focused pages were cited more in ChatGPT across 815,000 query-page pairs. *Headline only.*[^gm-shorter]
- **Long:** ChatGPT retrieves about 6× more pages than it cites (85 % of retrieved pages are never cited), about 30 domains take 67 % of citations per topic, and pages over 20,000 characters averaged 10 citations vs 2.39 for pages under 500. *Relayed from Growth Memo.*[^ignite-news]
- **Reading:** long pages offer more citable passages; focused pages answer one intent cleanly.
- **Do:** one topic per page, covered in depth — not thin pages, not catch-all guides ([comparison and guides](../page-types/comparison-and-guides.md)).

## How many prompt runs?
- **Few:** 3–5 runs per core prompt within a 24–72 h window, logged as counts. *Practitioner.*[^aleyda-prompts]
- **Many:** at a 50 % appearance rate, 5 runs leave ±44 points of error, 20 runs ±22, 100 runs ±10. *Statistics.*[^ipr-precision]
- **Do:** 3–5 runs to see whether you appear at all; ≥ 20 runs on the prompts where you'll act on a change ([diagnostic tests](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md#9-prompt-tracking-per-engine)).

## Fewer or more sources per answer?
- **Fewer:** domains per ChatGPT answer fell from 19.1 to 15.2 after one model release (4 March 2026). *Measured.*[^chatgpt-53]
- **More:** at high vs minimal reasoning, sources per answer rose from 2.6 to 4.5. *Measured, 100 prompts.*[^gm-reasoning]
- **Do:** the count depends on model and mode; pin both when tracking and mark model releases as events.

## Why is organic CTR falling?
- **AI Overviews:** a randomised field experiment found 38 % fewer organic clicks to external sites when an AIO appeared. *Relayed.*[^ignite-news] Pew (900 US adults, 68,879 searches, March 2025): users clicked a classic result in 8 % of visits with an AI summary vs 15 % without, clicked a link inside the summary in 1 %, and ended the session on the page in 26 % vs 16 %. *Measured.* The often-quoted "under 1 % click" misreads the 1 % summary-link figure.[^pew-aio-clicks]
- **Text ads:** in four US verticals (January 2025 vs 2026), text ads were the largest measurable driver of the organic decline, ahead of AI Overviews. *Measured, search extracts.*[^aleyda-ads]
- **Do:** split clicks into organic, paid and zero-click per query set, and AIO vs non-AIO queries, before blaming either ([search console](../measure/search-console.md), [traffic drop](../situations/traffic-drop.md)).

## How high is zero-click?
- **46.96 %** of 13.1 billion searches (Oct 2024 – Dec 2025, 94.5 % desktop) ended without a click. *Measured.*[^ipr-zero-click]
- **68.01 %** of US Google web searches in Jan–Apr 2026 (Similarweb panel, Google app excluded); Germany 62.1 %, UK 69.5 %. *Measured.*[^sp-zero-click-2026][^ignite-news]
- **Do:** a method difference, not a contradiction — quote any zero-click rate with its source and device mix.

## When did num=100 hit?
- Search Console impressions fell from 10 September 2025, mostly on desktop.[^brodie-num100] Marie Haynes dates the change "around September 11".[^mh-spam] Brodie Clark's note on the shutdown says 12 September.[^brodie-num100]
- **Do:** use 13 September 2025 as the first clean day for impression and position baselines ([search console](../measure/search-console.md)).

## How often do AI Overviews show?
- **29.9 %** of 10,000 US keywords, but 11.5 % of search volume (Authoritas, December 2024). *Relayed.*[^thrive-traffic]
- **20.5 %** of 146,122,391 US desktop results pages (September 2025): informational 21.4 %, commercial 4.3 %, transactional 2.1 %, Shopping category 3.2 %. *Measured, vendor data.*[^ah-aio-triggers]
- **39.4 %** of US desktop searches in June 2026 (Comscore). *Relayed.*[^ignite-news]
- **About 18 %** of UK queries in spring 2025, up from 4 % (Sistrix), with about 6 cited sources per AI Overview. *Relayed.*[^gn-aiyear]
- **Local queries:** 57 % showed an AI Overview or AI Mode (Whitespark, no method) *relayed*,[^thrive-ai-mode-local] vs 7.9 % of local searches with an AI Overview in the September 2025 crawl.[^ah-aio-triggers]
- **Branded queries:** 13.1 % in the September 2025 data,[^ah-aio-triggers] 73.3 % as the September 2026 monthly average.[^ah-aio-branded]
- **Reading:** different samples (keywords, SERPs, searches) and dates, and the rate is rising fast. The split by intent is consistent: guides and questions trigger AI Overviews far more often than category and product queries.
- **Do:** quote any rate with its source, date and query mix; measure your own share of AIO-triggering queries per intent.

## Out-of-stock pages: noindex or keep indexed?
- **Noindex or 410:** Botpresso recommends 410 for discontinued products, a conditional noindex for temporary stockouts, and noindexing listing pages below an item threshold (no value given), because stocked-out product and listing pages slip into Soft 404. *Practitioner, vendor.*[^bp-soft404]
- **Keep indexed with markup:** Google's product markup defines `OutOfStock`, `SoldOut`, `BackOrder` and `Discontinued` availability values, but the docs give no rule on whether to keep such pages indexed. *Official, definitions only.*[^g-product-snippet] Common practice keeps temporary stockouts indexable so they keep their rankings. *Inference.*
- **Do:** keep temporarily out-of-stock products indexable with the right availability value and useful alternatives on the page; 410 or 301 permanently discontinued products (301 when there's a close successor); consider noindex only for long stockouts; watch the Soft 404 count per page type ([product pages](../page-types/product-pages.md)).

## Do meta descriptions still matter?
- **Mostly not:** Google rewrites 62.78 % of meta descriptions (2020 data);[^ah-meta] ChatGPT's model ignores them.[^chatgpt-retrieval]
- **Sometimes they're all Gemini gets:** when Gemini grounds on Google, 7.6 % of excerpts were the meta description, and in 83 % of those the only text passed. Meta-only pages were cited 22 % of the time vs 72 % with body excerpts; category pages 6 % vs 58 %. Meta excerpts made up 43 % of homepage and 23 % of category excerpts but 3.8 % of articles. *Measured, Gemini API only, observational.*[^nz-meta] Natzir reads a meta-only excerpt as a symptom of body copy that doesn't match the query.
- **Do:** on category and home pages, write real meta descriptions (brands, ranges, prices, criteria) — and first give those pages visible body copy that answers their head queries ([category pages](../page-types/category-pages.md), [titles and snippets](../technical/titles-and-snippets.md)).

## How much of a page do AI engines read?
The figures describe different layers, not one number:
- **ChatGPT's search card:** title, URL and a ~202-character snippet from the H1 for each candidate. *Measured.*[^chatgpt-retrieval]
- **ChatGPT's fetched pages:** scored per chunk, the best chunk normalised to 1.0; pages not yet indexed are split into "hundreds of small fragments". *Network capture, one prompt, vendor author.*[^mt-chunks] OpenAI's grounding passes "…"-joined windows of 100 or 200 words. *Probing, one query.*[^dj-grounding]
- **Gemini's citations:** half of cited passages are 43 words or fewer, 80 % from the first half of the page. *Measured.*[^gemini-convo]
- **Google's grounding budget:** median 1,929 words per query, median 377 per source, built from ~15.5-word sentence chunks, levelling off at ~540 words per page. *Measured, private data.*[^dj-chunks]
- **Do:** put the answer, specs and differentiators in the first ~500 words, in self-contained sentences ([comparisons and guides](../page-types/comparison-and-guides.md)).

## Do AI crawlers use sitemaps?
- **No:** GPTBot followed internal links and never fetched sitemap-only URLs. *Measured, server logs.*[^chatgpt-retrieval]
- **Yes:** GPTBot found a new 60,000-page site through its XML sitemap within minutes, with no links — 29,000+ requests in 12 hours. *Measured, one site, vendor author.*[^mt-gptbot]
- **Reading:** both may be true for different sites, dates and crawl states.
- **Do:** keep sitemaps clean and complete, and still link every page you want found ([internal linking](../technical/internal-linking.md)).

## How often does ChatGPT search inside sites?
- **Often:** in the consumer app, `site:` sub-queries went from under 0.11 % of weekly queries (mid-May – late July 2026) to 26.2 % in the week of 10 August (3.9M of 14.9M), mostly aimed at official brand sites, docs, help centres and review platforms. *Measured, Peec AI vendor logs.*[^mt-sitequeries]
- **Rarely:** via OpenAI's API, `site:` share was 5–6 % on gpt-5.2/5.4 and 0.15 % on gpt-5.5; fan-outs per searched answer fell from 6.16 (March 2026) to 1.10 (August). Long ad-hoc prompts produced 7–91× more `site:` queries than short tracking prompts. *Measured, 196,692 API fan-outs.*[^dj-sitefanout]
- **Very often, on the newer model:** across 346 matched prompts, `site:` appeared in 21.6 % of fan-outs on GPT-5.5 and 76 % on GPT-5.6 Luna (ChatGPT's default since 9 July 2026); on non-brand prompts 20.6 % → 77.3 %, while sources consulted fell 43 %. *Measured.*[^seer-luna]
- **Reading:** app vs API, model version and prompt style explain most of the gap; Seer's GPT-5.5 figure is far above DEJAN's API figure for the same model.
- **Do:** make sure the facts a `site:` search would look for — specs, delivery, returns, prices — are on your own crawlable pages ([diagnostic tests](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md)).

## How long is RankEmbed's training window?
- **70 days** of search logs plus rater scores, per Marie Haynes' reading of the trial exhibits.[^mh-trial]
- **One month** of data, per Natzir Turrado's reading of the testimony.[^nz-qt]
- **Do:** nothing changes in practice; quote the primary exhibit, not either summary ([Google ranking](../how-it-works/google-ranking.md)).

## Do AI assistants render JavaScript?
- **No:** ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Gemini and Perplexity-User read static HTML on one test page. *One test page.*[^llm-crawler] No JavaScript executed across more than 500 million GPTBot fetches. *Measured, relayed.*[^ipr-cloaking]
- **Gemini, Copilot partly yes:** in a retest across three page types, ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity failed all three; Gemini read the content in all three; Copilot passed the two JavaScript tests and failed on WebGL. *Small test.*[^gg-js]
- **Reading:** Gemini answers from Google's index, which holds the rendered page, not from a live fetch. *Probing.*[^dj-notlive]
- **Do:** server-render prices, specs, links and copy; check each assistant with a first-and-last-paragraph read-back ([diagnostic tests](../measure/diagnostic-tests.md#4-fetch-a-page-the-way-an-ai-assistant-does)).

## How much do AI engines cite Reddit?
- **A lot:** Reddit appeared in ~62 % of responses to bottom-funnel prompts (January–February 2026). *Vendor data.*[^siege-bofu] Across models, Reddit was the most-cited source in Profound's 250M-response data. *Vendor data, conference talk.*[^lr-techseo] reddit.com took 17.9 % of AI Mode citations (US, September 2026). *Vendor data.*[^ah-ai-mode-domains]
- **Little:** Reddit was 2.0 % of 697,768 Google AI citations; OpenAI selected 0.61 % of Reddit retrievals vs 12.85 % for all sources; Anthropic cited none. *Measured, own prompts.*[^dj-reddit] Its share of ChatGPT citations fell from ~4.6 % to 0.23 % in August 2026. *Vendor logs.*[^mt-sitequeries]
- **Reading:** "share of responses that include Reddit", "share of citations" and "share of retrievals selected" are different metrics; engines differ; and ChatGPT changed in August 2026.
- **Do:** measure per engine and month; invest in forums only where threads already rank in Google for your queries ([third-party presence](../channels/third-party-presence.md)).

## ccTLD or subfolder for a new market?
- **ccTLDs:** beyond a few countries, separate country domains avoid the complexity of subfolders and subdomains; hreflang is "hints, not directives". *Practitioner, news sites.*[^gn-intl]
- **Follow the market:** one publisher launched a .de because "78% of the German SERP was already .de". *Conference talk.*[^lr-techseo]
- **Google:** the localisation docs read here give hreflang rules but no preference for a site structure. *Official.*[^g-hreflang]
- **Do:** check the ccTLD share in the target market's results before choosing; whichever structure, translate the full main content ([new market](../situations/new-market.md)).

## Does Discover follow Search rankings?
- **Google:** Discover has "minimal overlap with Search ranking". *Official statement, relayed.*[^gg-may2026]
- **Cases:** many sites hit in a core update lost both Search and Discover, some at the final tremor of the May 2026 rollout. *Practitioner cases.*[^gg-may2026]
- **Do:** expect Discover to move with core updates; track it separately ([Google Discover](../channels/google-discover.md)).

## Track answer position or presence?
- **Position matters:** score each mention for regularity, accuracy, prominence (listed first) and sentiment. *Practitioner framework.*[^cc-rapp]
- **Presence and share of voice:** answers are samples — the same brand list repeats less than once in 100 runs, and a single run's position is noise.[^nz-trackers][^ipr-precision] Visibility can rise while share of voice falls (38 % → 44 % visibility, 26 % → 21 % share of voice). *Case and illustration.*[^seer-visibility-lying]
- **Drop position:** SparkToro and Gumshoe ran 142 prompts 994 times; the same list in the same order took ~1,429 runs, while visibility share stayed stable. *Measured, vendor data.*[^sp-track-ai-visibility]
- **Do:** report presence % and share of voice as headline metrics, log accuracy and sentiment, and keep position as a secondary field ([AI visibility tracking](../measure/ai-visibility-tracking.md)).

## Do AI recommendations lift brand demand?
- **Yes, for large brands:** devices that saw an AI answer recommend a brand were more likely to visit it within 7 days (+7.2 % and +14.2 % for two US finance brands). *Measured, Similarweb panel, sample not stated.*[^sp-similarweb-ai-mentions]
- **No reliable link:** across 12 sites, AI visibility didn't correlate with branded search or Direct traffic. *Measured, relayed (Kevin Indig).*[^sp-ai-boondoggle]
- **Do:** track branded search, Direct and AI visibility side by side ([AI visibility tracking](../measure/ai-visibility-tracking.md)); a lift is plausible for well-known brands but not shown for smaller ones.

## How many searches reach AI Mode?
- **0.34 %** of US Google searches reached AI Mode in Jan–Apr 2026. *Measured, Similarweb panel.*[^sp-zero-click-2026]
- **Just over 1 %** of searches used the AI Mode tab in June 2025, also Similarweb ([Google AI features](../how-it-works/google-ai-features.md)).
- **Do:** a method and time difference — quote AI Mode share with its period and definition, and expect it to move.

## Embedding clusters or SERP clusters?
- **Embeddings:** cluster keywords by meaning with a fixed algorithm, the usual tool-based method ([keyword research](../channels/keyword-research.md)).
- **SERP overlap:** on real data, embedding clusters barely matched clusters built from shared ranking URLs (adjusted Rand index 0.04). *Measured, small sample.*[^mlseo-kw-clustering]
- **Do:** use embeddings to explore a keyword set; decide which keywords share a URL by SERP overlap.

## Is the 2 MB HTML limit enforced?
- **Yes:** Google's documentation states a 2 MB limit for HTML, and a forum report saw truncation at about 1.9 MB.[^dbb-2mb]
- **Not in tests:** URL Inspection live tests didn't truncate pages above 2 MB. *Vendor test, small sample.*[^dbb-2mb]
- **Do:** keep the main content and links well inside the first 2 MB of HTML ([technical foundations](../technical/technical-foundations.md#24-html-size-limits)).

## Update or merge first when pruning?
- **Update first:** the pruning order on [scaling content](../situations/scaling-content-and-templates.md#8-pruning-order) improves a page before merging it.
- **Merge first:** a rule-based audit template checks for duplicates (merge) before decay (update); the first matching rule wins. *Practitioner.*[^mlseo-content-audit]
- **Do:** settled by scope — merge true duplicates first, then update the pages that remain.
