# Page Types

What to do on each type of page: rules, a checklist, and the why with links to the evidence.

* [Category pages](category-pages.md) - What to do on a category or listing page — factual opening sentence, the searcher's exact terms in title and H1, one idea per passage, filter pages only with demand, crawlable pagination, internal links and section quality, the buyer's main uncertainty, consolidation, satisfaction per page type, listings near zero stock, body copy and a real meta description, customer vocabulary, URLs that mirror the taxonomy, links to complementary categories, footer category links and cross-sell blocks in `<aside>`, and feed-generated category templates audited before spam updates.
* [Comparison pages and guides](comparison-and-guides.md) - What to do on "X vs Y" pages, buying guides and product documentation — verdict first, comparison table and "best for", HTML not PDF, decision-stage over how-to, one topic in depth, no length targets, true dates and real refreshes, consolidation into one guide with anchors, link assets, FAQ content after FAQ rich results were dropped, self-contained answer-first passages, the first ~500 words, original statistics pages with sourced numbers and quotes, no "according to X" rankings of your own products, headlines and summaries for editorial guides, and the strip-the-logo test.
* [Homepage and brand pages](homepage-and-brand.md) - What to do on the homepage and brand-intent pages — verifiable proof points as text, one page per branded intent, brand queries that no longer guarantee a click, the merchant knowledge panel fed by Merchant Center and Business Profile, clicks without citations, brand identity in core updates, third-party listings that match the site, AI Overviews on branded queries, entity hygiene and Knowledge Graph facts, AI referrals landing on the homepage, brand-verification queries, homepage body copy and "need → brand" statements, short stable brand URLs, hallucinated variants of your hostname, brand display in results (favicon, site name, logo, OG image), and a brand canon of checkable facts with accuracy tracking, early facts for obscure questions, per-language anchor pages and the training cut-off.
* [Product pages](product-pages.md) - What to do on a product page — factual opening sentence, price, specs and stock as server-rendered text in one main container (never in CSS pseudo-content), model codes as visible text, descriptive titles and URLs, variant URLs and markup, the same facts as the feed, "will it fit?" and use-case copy tested with task-style prompts, reviews and Q&A, own images for Lens and indexing, the page as ad landing page, "confirm and go" for AI visitors, and out-of-stock and discontinued products.
