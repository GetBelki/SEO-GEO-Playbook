# Situations

Step-by-step playbooks for situations: a traffic drop, a migration, a new market, seasonal peaks, recovering from a core update.

* [Core update recovery](core-update-recovery.md) - What to do after losing visibility in a Google core update — confirm the dates and wait out mid-rollout tremors, compare clicks in clean windows, find the loss by directory and page type, compare with what winners and losers share (including intrusive ads and sister sites), treat indexing as a quality signal, tell spam and manual actions from core (feed-generated templates, site reputation abuse), avoid rewrites that only win briefly, watch brand demand and unlinked mentions, and expect the loss to carry into AI answers.
* [Site migration and URL renames](migration.md) - How to rename URLs, move a domain or relaunch a site without losing search, ads or AI visibility — Google's official move rules, same-day internal links and sitemaps, a control set, redirects that keep query strings, feed and ad URLs, pre-launch checks on staging, a separate bucket for parameter and facet URLs, short stable core URLs, daily per-URL and per-template monitoring afterwards, signals derived from one record so sitemaps, hreflang, canonicals and feeds don't drift, and the Removals tool only as a stopgap for a leaked staging or login host.
* [Entering a new market](new-market.md) - Launching a shop in a new country or language — hreflang and x-default, visible-language content, English fan-out vs local sources, native prompt sets, each market's marketplaces, EEA vs non-EEA surfaces, feeds and local listings, no IP auto-redirects, AI Overview launches per market, country controls in measurement, hosting location (not a geotargeting signal), machine-translated Reddit threads competing in Google AI surfaces, assistants that ignore hreflang, per-language anchor pages, and choosing ccTLD or subfolder by what the market's results show.
* [Scaling content and templates](scaling-content-and-templates.md) - Programmatic and AI-assisted pages at scale — Google's scaled content abuse policy, what AI-written content data shows, indexation as the quality gate, template inventory and ownership, tickets with money impact, per-template sample crawls, soft 404 rules for out-of-stock products and thin listings, repeated template phrasing and the strip-the-logo test, the pruning order, why not to atomise topics into micro-pages, the August 2026 spam update's feed-generated templates, and programmatic guardrails (minimum data per page, generation from database fields, fact checks, scheduled pruning).
* [Seasonal peaks](seasonal-peaks.md) - Preparing for Black Friday, Christmas and other peaks — evergreen URLs with the year in title and H1, pages kept between seasons, publishing ~6 months ahead, branded promo intents, Merchant Center promotions, measurement around peaks, update rollouts that land in peak season, and real dated refreshes for AI rerankers that favour recent pages.
* [Organic traffic drop](traffic-drop.md) - A decision flow for a fall in organic or AI-referred traffic — rule out measurement breaks, date it against Google updates, split by device, country, brand and query type, then test directories, lost queries, satisfaction, indexing, AI Overview launches per market, AI referral shifts by page type, platform-side citation shifts such as ChatGPT dropping Reddit, structural surface changes, branded CTR lost to a missing favicon, country-section losses from cross-country canonicalisation, paid CTR falling too, skewed industry decline figures, mid-rollout tremors and security tools serving 4xx to bots — and don't disavow tool-flagged links.
